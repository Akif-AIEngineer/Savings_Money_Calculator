# 💰 Corporate Income & Savings Calculator 📈💸

**Creator:** 🩷 MD AKIF ANNU 🩷
**Goal:** To calculate exact monthly savings after tracking total income and deducting fixed expenses like loan repayments and recharges. 🎯📊

### 🟨 Asli Kaam (Features) 🚀:
* **Income Tracking:** Multiple tution incomes ko ek jagah automatically total karta hai. 💵➕
* **Expense Management:** Phone loan aur recharge jaise fixed kharcho ko minus karta hai. 📉💳
* **Financial Modeling:** Final Savings Money ka exact result nikal kar aage ki financial planning meinn help karta hai. 🏦🏆

**Business Value:** Ye ek basic financial model hai jo sikhata hai ki company ya individual apne cash flow aur profits ko data ke through kaise manage kar sakte hain. 🏢📈
